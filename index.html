Yeeps Channel Fundings

Yeeps Channel Fundings supports the creative projects and productions behind Yeeps Channel. Funding helps bring new shows, original content, studio productions, and films to life while giving creators the resources they need to keep producing.
Our funding supports three major areas of Yeeps Channel:

Yeeps Channel Studios

A production space focused on creating and developing new Yeeps Channel programming.

Yeeps Channel Originals

Original shows and productions created specifically for Yeeps Channel.

Yeeps Channel Films

Film projects and larger productions developed under the Yeeps Channel brand. Together, these divisions help expand what Yeeps Channel produces and give new ideas a place to grow.

Why us?

We believe good ideas deserve the resources to become real productions. We help provide resources for projects with creative potential. Fundings allows us to develop new shows, films, and studio productions. Projects supported through Yeeps Channel Fundings become part of a larger creative network.
We focus on supporting the people and ideas behind each production. Every project helps expand Yeeps Channel and its programming lineup.
