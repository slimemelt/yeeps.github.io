Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains what information Yeeps Channel collects from people in our community and how we use it. We only collect what we need to run Yeeps Channel.

1. Information we collect

When you take part in the community, we may collect:

  • Your Discord username and ID
  • Messages and activity in our Discord servers, which we log for moderation
  • Moderation records, such as warnings, timeouts and bans, with the reason and date
  • Anything you send us when you contact us or appeal an action

When you apply or join the Creator Program, we may also collect:

  • Your channel name
  • Your YouTube channel link and links to your Yeeps videos
  • Basic channel statistics
  • Your answers on the application

2. How we use it

We use this information to:

  • Run and moderate the community and enforce our rules
  • Review applications and manage membership
  • Contact you about community and program updates
  • Air and promote creators' content on Yeeps Channel
  • Improve our services

3. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it only:

  • With administrators
  • When the law requires it
  • With services we need to run the community, such as Discord

4. Public information

Public YouTube information, such as video titles, may be used in promotions for creators in the Creator Program.

5. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we store. No internet service can guarantee complete security.

6. Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the information we hold about you. If you leave, we will stop using it, except where we need to keep a record to enforce our rules, such as a ban.

7. Children's privacy

Yeeps Channel does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we find that someone is under 13, or under the minimum age in their country, we will remove them.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Updates will be posted with a new date at the top.

9. Contact

For questions or requests about your information:

  • Email: rio@bloomnetworks.net
  • Discord: message a moderator in the Yeeps Channel server
